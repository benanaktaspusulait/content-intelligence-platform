# Root-cause audit

1. `optimization_plans.py::SourceOwnedPlans/ExecutionChoice/SYSTEM`: the provider can select only a catalog ID, framing and motion, plus explanation. It cannot specify spatial layout or contact choreography. Both opening/escalation slots are required regardless of available evidence.
2. `optimization_plans.py::compile_plan`: source narrative is preserved, but execution compilation adds generic “visible together”, “focus then” and “hold two/four/eight” annotations. Explanations are not an execution plan. This is why plan-first alone did not demonstrate stronger staging.
3. `optimization_distinctness.py::validate_execution_candidates`: material difference is primarily timing/framing/motion signature. The annotation vocabulary permits camera language rather than spatial choreography. A small but important same-camera contact correction is rejected; a framing/timing combination may pass.
4. `optimization_comparison.py::assess_comparison`: legacy materiality uses camera motion or framing plus timing, and a compiled legacy plan is treated as material. Benefits correctly remain unproven, but diagnosis-to-change proof is missing.
5. `WorkflowService::generateCreativeOptimization/assessOptimizationComparison/revalidateCreativeOptimization` and frontend `canSelectOptimizationCandidate`: exact two-candidate assumptions reject one defensible result and prevent clean no-improvement results.
6. The legacy role instructions preserve narrative wording and essential anchors. Preserving story is appropriate, but restricting editable execution to camera words collapses meaningful production freedom. V2 preserves source narrative while compiling a separate spatial/choreographic execution layer.
7. BUILD_PROMPT already has source-bound action/state/staging/consequence and risk contracts. V2 reuses its source philosophy, existing IR/parser, plan-first protected_contract, profile normalization, paid adapter, durable attempts, recovery, canonical review, comparison lineage, immutable approval and Step 6/F8 governance. It does not dispatch a second BUILD_PROMPT call.
8. Model capability is not live-verified by this work. Server-side explicit model/rate/budget checks and zero automatic provider retry remain. Offline evidence cannot establish that a real model will choose strong plans.

Smallest architecture change: evolve the existing PROMPT_OPTIMIZATION entry point with a versioned V2 strategy, source-derived diagnosis, explicit production operations, bounded in-response critique/revision and compiled change contracts. Preserve legacy contracts for historical recovery and comparisons.
