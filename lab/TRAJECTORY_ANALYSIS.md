# Trajectory Analysis

Derived trajectories calculate interval velocity, acceleration, engagement ratios, follow conversion, time to internal performance bands, and country-share movement. Supported descriptive labels include `EARLY_STALL`, `SLOW_GROWTH`, `SECOND_WAVE`, `MULTI_WAVE`, `PERSISTENT_GROWTH`, and `LATE_BREAKOUT`.

Labels describe observed Pompom data. They do not claim knowledge of private platform distribution stages. Every derived point links back to normalised observations.
