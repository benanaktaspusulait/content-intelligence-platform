1. seed42-001 / The Wrinkle Has to Go Somewhere

ideaId: seed42-001
title: The Wrinkle Has to Go Somewhere
protagonistSuggestion: Mimi
everydayGoal: Iron a wrinkled shirt flat.
primaryObject: Clothes iron
normalExpectation: Pressing an iron over a shirt removes its creases while the ironing board provides flat support.
impossibleRule: Whenever the iron presses a crease out of the shirt, an identical crease forms in the ironing board directly beneath that crease.
visibleTrigger: The iron presses a raised shirt crease flat.
whyThisObject: The iron needs firm, flat support beneath the fabric; its ordinary smoothing action progressively folds that supporting surface.
goalRuleRelationship: Every crease Mimi removes from the shirt makes the board less able to support the next ironing stroke.
frameZeroDescription: Mimi stands at an ironing board with an iron planted on a shirt. One sleeve is smooth above a sharply zigzagged section of board; the remaining shirt is heavily wrinkled on the still-flat section. The board's padded edge visibly follows the deep zigzag beneath the smooth sleeve.
Beat 1:
  strategy: Mimi slides the iron across the shirt's wrinkled middle.
  visibleConsequence: The shirt's middle becomes smooth while the board beneath it buckles into a broad ridge, tipping the remaining wrinkles toward Mimi.
  newInformation: A larger shirt crease produces the same larger fold in the supporting board.
Beat 2:
  strategy: Mimi pulls the wrinkled hem onto the board's remaining flat end and presses it.
  visibleConsequence: The hem flattens, but the board's end folds upward, leaving the shirt draped between two raised sections.
  newInformation: Moving to unused support only relocates the accumulating board creases.
Beat 3:
  strategy: Mimi stretches the last wrinkled sleeve across the board's central ridge and presses along it.
  visibleConsequence: The sleeve smooths as a new crease folds the ridge into a compact accordion, leaving almost no level working surface.
  newInformation: The existing board folds remain while each newly removed shirt crease adds another.
Beat 4:
  strategy: Mimi presses the final cuff crease against the accordion's small flat top, then lifts the shirt by its shoulders.
  visibleConsequence: Mimi holds up a perfectly smooth shirt above an ironing board crumpled into a giant shirt-shaped set of wrinkles.
  newInformation: The shirt is flat because its entire collection of creases now occupies the board.
finalPayoff: Mimi holds up a perfectly smooth shirt above an ironing board crumpled into a giant shirt-shaped set of wrinkles.
suggestedPayoffFamily: Solution-created problem
loopPotential: No reset; the smooth shirt and crumpled board remain.
environment: A quiet laundry corner with one ironing board and clear floor space.
dialogueNeed: NONE
providerRationale: Ironing depends on flat support. Transferring each removed crease into that support lets successful smoothing progressively destroy the working surface.
durationSeconds: 15
