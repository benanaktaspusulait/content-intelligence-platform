ideaId: seed42-01
title: A Very Square Spill
protagonistSuggestion: Mimi
everydayGoal: Clear a puddle from the floor so the door can open.
primaryObject: Sponge
normalExpectation: A sponge absorbs spilled water and releases liquid when squeezed.
impossibleRule: Whenever Mimi squeezes the wet sponge, its absorbed water drops out as one rigid transparent cube of the same volume.
visibleTrigger: Mimi compresses the visibly swollen sponge between her palms.
whyThisObject: The sponge must be squeezed to regain its capacity, but that normally useful operation deposits solid water blocks in the doorway she is trying to clear.
goalRuleRelationship: Absorbing the puddle removes the liquid obstruction while squeezing creates a solid obstruction in the same working area.
frameZeroDescription: Mimi kneels beside a partly open door whose bottom edge meets a broad puddle. Her hands compress a swollen sponge above a transparent water cube resting in the puddle; the cube has flat faces and sharp square corners, with a wet imprint beneath it. Her shoulder is against the door, and the cube occupies its opening arc.
BEAT 1
  strategy: Mimi pushes the door with her shoulder while finishing the squeeze.
  visibleConsequence: The door stops against the water cube, leaving only a narrow gap.
  newInformation: The extracted water is solid enough to block the door.
BEAT 2
  strategy: She slides the cube away from the door and uses the sponge to absorb the remaining broad puddle.
  visibleConsequence: The door's opening arc becomes dry, but the sponge bulges between her hands.
  newInformation: The liquid can be cleared, provided she finds somewhere else to squeeze.
BEAT 3
  strategy: Mimi reaches through the narrow door gap and squeezes the sponge on the other side.
  visibleConsequence: A larger water cube drops outside the room, directly behind the door.
  newInformation: Moving the squeeze outside has placed the new block on the opposite side of the same door.
BEAT 4
  strategy: She stands on the dry floor and confidently pulls the door open.
  visibleConsequence: The door wedges between the two transparent water cubes, framing Mimi in a perfectly dry doorway too narrow to pass through.
  newInformation: She has removed the entire puddle but converted it into a solid doorstop on both sides.
finalPayoff: The door wedges between the two transparent water cubes, framing Mimi in a perfectly dry doorway too narrow to pass through.
suggestedPayoffFamily: Solution-created problem
loopPotential: No reset; the dry floor and solid water blocks remain.
environment: A small tiled entryway with an inward-opening door.
dialogueNeed: NONE
providerRationale: The sponge's required absorb-and-squeeze cycle converts a spreading liquid obstacle into compact solid obstacles. Moving the squeeze across the threshold changes where the same consequence lands.
durationSeconds: 15
