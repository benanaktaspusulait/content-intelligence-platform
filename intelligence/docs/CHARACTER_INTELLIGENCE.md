# Character Intelligence

Characters are configured data, never code constants. A video may have one primary and multiple secondary characters with role and nullable screen/action/speaking shares.

Reports must stratify character evidence by platform, series, creative engine, and character pair. Raw means are shown alongside sample size and uncertainty. Small samples require shrinkage toward the relevant platform/series baseline.

`NEW` and `UNTESTED` mean unknown, not weak. Opportunity ranking combines evidence scarcity, uncertainty, creative fit, platform evidence, and expected information gain. The planner preserves explicit capacity for new and limited-data characters.
