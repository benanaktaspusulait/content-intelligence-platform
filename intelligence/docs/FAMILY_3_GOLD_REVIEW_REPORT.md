# FAMILY 3 — Goal + Realization Gold Review Report

**Status:** HUMAN-REVIEW PREPARATION — ENGINE NOT CHANGED

**Golden Set:** `POMPOM_GOLDEN_V1`

**Scope:** Goal explicitness, local obstruction, intended effect, and observable realization/belief-state change only.

## Semantic Contract

```text
EXPLICIT
    The prompt directly states what the character wants or names the intended outcome.

IMPLICIT_BUT_OBSERVABLE
    The character's action and obstruction make the local goal and intended effect readable
    without a direct goal sentence.

NOT_ESTABLISHED
    The source does not establish a reliable local goal or realization for the dimension.

UNKNOWN
    The source or current evidence is insufficient to decide without inventing semantics.
```

A realization does not require dialogue such as “I understand.” Observable evidence is sufficient when the character tests/observes the object, the object appears normal or controlled, and the character changes belief, relaxes, smiles, celebrates, or deliberately chooses the next action.
