# Luca’s Missing Shoe

TYPE: Social Short / English Mini-Sketch
DURATION: 15–18 seconds
FORMAT: 9:16 vertical
STYLE: Pompom Hills 3D
TARGET: Ages 6–10 + adults

CHARACTERS:
- Luca
- Kiko

LOCATION:
Opa’s Tree — entrance / getting-ready area

ENGLISH FOCUS:
- “Where is my shoe?”
- “Is it under the table?”
- “Behind the chair?”
- “Oh.”

CORE IDEA:
Luca is ready to leave but only has one shoe.
Kiko helps him search in obvious places.
The missing shoe turns out to have been hidden in the funniest possible place.

MANDATORY SPOKEN DIALOGUE / LIP-SYNC:
All lines are REQUIRED.

00.0–03.0s
Luca stands ready to go with one shoe on and one socked foot.

Luca:
“Where is my shoe?”

03.0–06.0s
Kiko looks under a nearby table.

Kiko:
“Is it under the table?”

Luca:
“No.”

06.0–10.0s
Kiko checks behind a chair.

Kiko:
“Behind the chair?”

Luca:
“No.”

10.0–14.0s
Luca scratches his head.

His missing shoe suddenly falls out from inside his hat or from where it was somehow sitting on top of his head.

14.0–18.0s
Luca looks down at it.

Luca:
“Oh.”

Kiko gives him a silent amused look.

VISUAL RULE:
The shoe reveal must be very clear and immediate.

AVOID:
complex search montage, too many locations, extra objects, narration, subtitles.
