# FAMILY 4 — Mechanic / Fake Resolution / Payoff Gold Review

**Status:** HUMAN-REVIEW PREPARATION — ENGINE NOT CHANGED

**Golden Set:** `POMPOM_GOLDEN_V1`

**Scope:** Central mechanic, character interaction, fake resolution, same-mechanic recurrence, final payoff, and causal connection between them.

## Semantic Contract

```text
central mechanic
    one established physical/comedic rule that organizes the source

fake resolution
    temporary normal-looking or successful state that the source later reverses

recurrence
    the established mechanic returns after the apparent resolution

payoff
    final consequence that is causally derived from the established mechanic
```

Important separations:

```text
temporary normal state != mechanic inconsistency
new consequence from same rule != new mechanic
payoff != merely a different final image
recurrence != automatically a loop
```
