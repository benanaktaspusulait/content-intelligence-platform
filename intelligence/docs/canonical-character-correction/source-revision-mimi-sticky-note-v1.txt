Already mid-action, Mimi, the light-blue rabbit, tries to place one sticky note on the toy cabinet's empty front panel, but it clings to Mimi's fingertip while the cabinet stays untouched. Mimi tries to peel the note away with the free hand, but it splits into two notes, one on each hand. Mimi tries to press both notes onto the cabinet, but they spring back and double into four notes. Mimi tries to push the four notes off against the cabinet door edge, but they split into eight and cover Mimi's arms and face. Mimi sees the four-to-eight change finish, realizes that every removal or transfer attempt multiplies the notes, deliberately stops struggling, and turns the still note-covered face toward the mirror. Mimi remains covered until a sneeze scatters the notes onto the cabinet. Mimi is relieved, but the cabinet remains messy.
