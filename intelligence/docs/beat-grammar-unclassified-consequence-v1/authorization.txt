======================================================================

Beat Grammar currently uses a consequence taxonomy derived largely from deterministic generation.

Semantic candidates may contain clear visible consequences that do not map to existing consequence labels.

Example situation:

semantic beat explicitly says:

"the paint on the wall changes to the opposite color"

or

"the object transfers its state to another surface"

The consequence is clearly present.

But if consequence classification returns UNKNOWN, Beat Grammar may currently treat the beat as unsupported/broken.

This repeats the previously solved evidence problem at a lower layer.
