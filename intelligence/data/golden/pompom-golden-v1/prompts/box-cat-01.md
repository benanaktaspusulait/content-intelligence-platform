# Luca vs Box Cat — OpenArt / Seedance 2.0 Mini

## Core concept
Luca wants to use an empty toy box.

**Single animal rule:** whenever the box becomes empty and usable, the cat claims it again.

## Prompt
Colorful high-quality stylized 3D preschool animation in the official Pompom Hills style. 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, one continuous fixed-camera shot in a warm simple playroom. Keep Luca, one rigid toy box and one stylized cat clearly visible. No cuts, no zoom, no shake.

Use the official Luca design exactly: messy dark brown hair, green/cream mountain shirt, green shorts, orange sneakers. Use one cute stylized cat with a simple smooth coat, low-detail fur, calm expression.

### Frame zero — conflict already happening
Start with Luca already reaching toward the toy box, but the cat is already curled up inside it. Luca's hand is paused just above the rim and he is looking at the cat.

### Attempt 1 — clear the box
Luca gently gestures the cat out of the box without touching it. The cat steps out. Luca turns back toward the box. The cat calmly steps back inside before Luca can use it. Luca gives a tiny puzzled look.

### Attempt 2 — move the box
Luca lifts the rigid empty box, moves it a short distance, and sets it down. He turns his head away for a brief moment. When he looks back, the cat is already sitting inside again.

### Attempt 3 — guard the box
Luca stays beside the empty box and watches carefully. The cat waits nearby. Luca looks away for just a moment toward the shelf. When he looks back, the cat has claimed the box again.

### Fake win + final payoff
The cat finally stays a short distance away. Luca sees the empty box and gives a small confident smile. He bends toward it to use it. Just before his hands reach the box, the cat makes one short calm move into the box and curls up again. Luca's hands stop in the air. Cat looks completely comfortable. Hard cut.

## Performance
Luca stays energetic, confident and determined. Cat stays calm and innocent. Comedy comes from Luca's serious effort versus the cat's complete certainty that the box belongs to it.

## Guardrails
- one Luca only
- one cat only
- one rigid toy box only
- same rule throughout: EMPTY BOX -> CAT CLAIMS BOX
- no chasing
- no jumping onto Luca
- no complex cat acrobatics
- no child-cat body contact
- no realistic fur simulation
- fixed camera
- one continuous take
- hard cut on Luca's frozen hands
