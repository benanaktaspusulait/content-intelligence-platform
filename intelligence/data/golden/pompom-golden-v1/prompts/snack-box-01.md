# Mimi and the Snack Box That Keeps Changing

TYPE: Social Short / Reel
DURATION: 15 seconds
FORMAT: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 24 fps
MODE: Frame to Video, one continuous shot
PRODUCTION MODE: FRESH QUALITY-RESET SHOT

CHARACTER:
Mimi

LOCATION:
Pompom Hills — picnic table / snack table area

LANGUAGE:
Very limited English dialogue.

MANDATORY SPOKEN LINES:
- Mimi: "Snack time!"
- Mimi: "What?"
- Mimi: "There!"

CORE IDEA:
Mimi wants a cracker from a snack box, but every time she opens it, something different is inside.
She tests the box again and again, then thinks she has solved it.
At the very end, the box still wins.

CHARACTER LOCK:
Preserve Mimi exactly:
- pastel-blue bunny
- white belly / muzzle
- long floppy ears
- green eyes
- pink nose
- yellow shirt
- soft rounded Pompom Hills design

LOCATION LOCK:
Use a simple cute snack setup:
- low table
- cheerful background
- a few picnic or snack details
- bright daylight
- clean readable space

BOX LOCK:
Exactly one snack box with a lid.
The box stays in the same place.
It never grows, moves away, or becomes scary.
Its only trick is that contents change between openings.

CAMERA:
Locked medium-wide vertical composition.
Keep visible:
- Mimi full or near full body
- full snack box
- tabletop

MANDATORY SPOKEN DIALOGUE / LIP-SYNC LOCK:
Speech is REQUIRED.
Mimi must audibly say:
1. "Snack time!" around 00.5s
2. "What?" around 04.5s
3. "There!" around 10.5s

ACTION TIMING:
00.0–02.0s
Mimi happily reaches for the box and opens it.
She expects a cracker.
Inside is an apple instead.
Mimi says: "Snack time!"

02.0–05.0s
Mimi closes and reopens the box.
Now there is a banana or spoon inside.
Mimi blinks and says: "What?"

05.0–08.0s
Mimi closes and opens it again.
Now a silly wrong item appears, like a sock or block.
Mimi becomes determined.

08.0–11.5s
Mimi turns the box slightly, taps it, closes it carefully, then opens it.
Finally, the cracker appears.
Mimi points proudly and says: "There!"

11.5–13.5s
Mimi reaches in to grab the cracker.
The lid suddenly flips shut or the cracker pops back down out of reach.

13.5–15.0s
Mimi reopens the box and sees another wrong item again.
She reacts and immediately leans in for another try, matching the opening rhythm for loop.

SOUND:
Small lid open/close sounds, light table taps, cute object swap sounds.
No heavy music.
Dialogue must be clear.

AVOID:
extra characters, spooky magic, talking box, scary object changes, crying, long final hold, text, subtitles, logo, watermark, cuts, zoom.
