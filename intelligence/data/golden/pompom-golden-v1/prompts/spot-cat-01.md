# Kiko vs Spot-Stealing Cat — OpenArt / Seedance 2.0 Mini

## Core concept
Kiko wants to sit down.

**Single animal rule:** whenever Kiko truly commits to sitting in an empty spot, the cat claims that exact spot first.

## Prompt
Colorful high-quality stylized 3D preschool animation in the official Pompom Hills style. 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, one continuous fixed-camera shot in a simple cozy room with three clearly readable open sitting spots on the floor/low seating area. Keep geometry simple and uncluttered. No cuts, no zoom, no shake.

Use the official Kiko design exactly: brown hair in two buns, large brown eyes, coral/pink long-sleeve top, white shorts, pink shoes. Use one cute stylized cat only, smooth low-detail coat, calm expression.

### Frame zero — conflict already happening
Start with Kiko already lowering herself toward an empty spot. At the same moment, the cat is curling into that exact spot just before Kiko sits. Kiko is visibly caught mid-squat.

### Attempt 1 — switch spot
Kiko immediately changes to a second nearby sitting spot. As soon as she clearly commits downward, the cat makes one short move and settles into that second spot first. Kiko stops safely and gives a suspicious side-eye.

### Attempt 2 — opposite spot
Kiko walks to a third open spot on the other side. She checks the cat, then starts to sit. The cat calmly reaches the target first and curls up. Kiko straightens and gives a tiny determined nod.

### Attempt 3 — decoy
Kiko pretends to sit in one spot, then quickly changes direction and commits to another. The cat ignores the fake and claims Kiko's real target spot just before she sits.

### Fake win + final payoff
The cat is looking away. Kiko slowly approaches the last empty spot. The cat does not move. Kiko gives a tiny proud smile and begins lowering herself. At the exact moment she truly commits, the cat makes one final short move into the spot and curls up. Kiko freezes in a squat just above the floor. She looks down. Cat looks up, perfectly comfortable. Hard cut.

## Performance
Kiko stays curious, kind, determined and slightly serious. Cat is never aggressive or smug; simply calm and certain.

## Guardrails
- one Kiko only
- one cat only
- no pillows or extra active props
- same rule throughout: SIT COMMITMENT -> CAT CLAIMS TARGET
- no Kiko-cat contact
- no falling
- no jumping cat
- no running
- simple short cat repositioning only
- fixed camera
- one continuous take
- hard cut on Kiko's suspended squat
