# Kiko and the Lamp That Hates Being Watched

TYPE: Social Short / Reel
DURATION: 15 seconds
FORMAT: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 24 fps
MODE: Frame to Video, one continuous shot
PRODUCTION MODE: FRESH QUALITY-RESET SHOT

CHARACTER:
Kiko

LOCATION:
Opa’s Tree — learning room / reading corner

LANGUAGE:
Very limited English dialogue.

MANDATORY SPOKEN LINES:
- Kiko: "Hmm?"
- Kiko: "I saw that!"
- Kiko: "Aha!"

CORE IDEA:
Kiko turns on a lamp.
When she looks at it, it behaves normally.
When she looks away, it switches off.
Kiko tests the lamp, thinks she has caught it, but the gag spreads to another light source.

CHARACTER LOCK:
Preserve Kiko exactly:
- dark hair in two buns
- brown eyes
- coral top
- white shorts
- coral shoes
- smart, playful, observant acting

LOCATION LOCK:
Use a cozy treehouse reading corner:
- table or reading chair
- one main lamp
- warm wood textures
- soft books / learning details
- calm interior lighting

LAMP LOCK:
Exactly one main table lamp or reading lamp.
It does not move.
It does not talk.
Its trick is only this:
when watched, it behaves;
when unwatched, it switches off or on at the wrong time.

CAMERA:
Locked medium-wide vertical framing.
Keep Kiko and lamp visible together.

MANDATORY SPOKEN DIALOGUE / LIP-SYNC LOCK:
Speech is REQUIRED.
Kiko must audibly say:
1. "Hmm?" around 01.0s
2. "I saw that!" around 07.0s
3. "Aha!" around 10.5s

ACTION TIMING:
00.0–02.0s
Kiko switches on the lamp and starts to read or look at the table.
She turns her head away for a moment.
The lamp switches off.
Kiko turns back and says: "Hmm?"

02.0–05.0s
Kiko switches it on again and stares at it.
It stays on.
She slowly looks away.
It switches off again.

05.0–08.0s
Kiko quickly spins back and points at it.
She says: "I saw that!"

08.0–11.5s
Kiko gets clever.
She uses a small mirror or reflective object to “watch” the lamp indirectly.
The lamp stays on.
Kiko smiles proudly and says: "Aha!"

11.5–13.5s
Just as Kiko celebrates, a second small light in the room flickers on or off instead.
Kiko looks toward it, surprised.

13.5–15.0s
When Kiko looks back at the main lamp, it is off again.
She lunges back toward it, matching the opening rhythm for loop.

SOUND:
Soft room ambience, light click sounds, tiny electric switch sounds.
No scary sound design.
Dialogue must be clearly audible.

AVOID:
horror lamp, flickering horror room, monster light, darkness scare, extra characters, crying, subtitles, logo, watermark, cuts, zoom.
